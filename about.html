HELLO & WELCOME

Why I created LifeSync

I’m studying Computer Technology and developing my skills in web design and software development. For my capstone project, I wanted to create something useful—an application that could help people organize the different parts of everyday life.

Between work, school, personal schedules, and daily responsibilities, it can be easy to lose track of things. LifeSync is my idea for bringing those everyday tasks together in one convenient place.

My goal is to make the application easy to understand, simple to navigate, and useful for students, working adults, and anyone trying to stay organized.

“Technology should make everyday life easier, not more complicated.”
